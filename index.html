what we do

venture architecture

We take an idea from a rough hypothesis to a validated concept — problem framing, product definition, prototyping and user research, ending in a specification a team can build from.

product development

We design and build digital products ourselves, or alongside the teams we work with — mobile apps, web products and the services behind them.

investment

We invest in and co-found early-stage digital companies, usually where we can contribute product and operational work rather than capital alone.

consultancy

We advise founders and companies on product strategy, digital business models, and how to get from an idea to something people actually use.

current work

in development · ios

Cooked

A cooking app that turns the screenshots people already take — recipe reels, recipe pages, notes from a chat — into structured recipes in a personal cookbook, and keeps a record of what was actually cooked. Currently in development, with a public beta planned for 2026.

in concept · ai · mcp

Meanwhile

An AI-powered universal inbox that lets users forward emails, messages and documents directly into their existing ChatGPT, Claude and other AI workflows. By making content accessible through MCP, it eliminates manual copy-pasting and keeps relevant information available across AI projects. Currently in concept development, with an MVP focused on email forwarding and AI integration.
